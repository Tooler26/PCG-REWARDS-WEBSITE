Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

Early information for customers and businesses interested in the programme.

What is PCG REWARDS?

A rewards and loyalty programme in development, intended to connect customers with participating shops through points and voucher codes.

How will I check my points balance?

The planned first customer-facing channel is WhatsApp. The private website dashboard also provides a place to view a wallet balance when your account is linked to the rewards ledger.

What is a voucher code?

A voucher code is intended to be issued by an approved participating shop and used according to programme rules. Never buy a code from an unofficial source or share account security details.

Can any shop join?

Businesses can express interest using the application form. Applications are subject to eligibility, review, approval, and final programme terms.

Is the service live now?

The website and WhatsApp integration are being prepared. Features should not be considered live until official launch information confirms availability.

How is my information handled?

Only provide information through official channels. Before launch, the programme owner must confirm the final privacy notice, contact details, retention practices, and applicable legal requirements.

Programme and shop eligibility

Participation is subject to shop approval, customer eligibility, published rules and service availability. An application does not guarantee acceptance. Do not represent the programme as live until launch readiness is confirmed.

Tokens and points

Shop tokens are linked to a specific approved shop and carry a value of 10, 15 or 20 points. Each token is unique and can be redeemed once only. The central system records redemption so another device should receive an already-used/unavailable response. Expired, cancelled, already-used tokens and tokens from inactive shops cannot be reused. Keep unused tokens private. Points are not cash unless official programme terms expressly say otherwise.

Rewards, errors and complaints

Rewards depend on the published catalogue, shop rules, provider availability and confirmed funding. Suspected fraud may be investigated; corrections should be recorded. Contact PCG REWARDS promptly about a disputed transaction.

Relevant South African laws

Depending on actual operations, relevant legislation may include POPIA, the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002, the Companies Act 71 of 2008 where applicable, and applicable tax, advertising, direct-marketing, electronic-communications and contract rules. Sector-specific laws may also apply based on the rewards and services offered.
